The Watch List

Filter by genre, search a title or actor, tick things off once you've watched them, and jot down who recommended each one. Your changes save automatically.

Streaming spots are best-effort for South Africa — Showmax, Prime Video, Netflix and Apple TV+ reshuffle their catalogues often, so if something's moved, JustWatch's ZA page (justwatch.com/za) will have the current home. A few titles (marked "unclear") I couldn't confidently identify from the name alone — worth double-checking the exact one you meant. Trailer links open a YouTube search for the title rather than one fixed video, so they stay current even after a trailer gets taken down or re-uploaded.
